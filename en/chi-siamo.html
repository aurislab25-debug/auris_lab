About us

Every bag is unique, handmade, one piece at a time.

Auri's Lab was born in Turin from a passion for crochet and for work done calmly, without rushing. We don't mass-produce: every bag starts from a ball of yarn — mostly lycra, sometimes a cotton blend — and hours of handwork, stitch by stitch.

The name combines the idea of attentive listening — "auris", Latin for ear — with that of a small workshop, a "lab" where every piece is designed and made individually. It's slow work by choice: a minimum of two weeks for every bag, because care can't be rushed.

Not every bag stays available for long. Once a piece is ready, it stays in the catalog until it finds the right person — then work moves on to the next one, different from the last because it's handmade, and so never quite identical.

"A handmade bag carries the time of the person who made it. That's not a flaw to hide: it's the point."

Craftsmanship

A crochet technique passed down and refined over time, applied to a product made for everyday use.

Slowness

No mass production, no endless stock. Every piece takes its time, and it shows in the result.

Uniqueness

Small variations in thread tension, shape, colour: they're the signature of handmade work, not a flaw.
